## 4. Your margin reality check

| Item | Wholesale cost | Kampala retail (your sell) | Markup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain t-shirt | UGX 6,000–10,600 | UGX 25,000 | **2.4–4×** ✅ |
| Textured polo | UGX 10,600 (MOQ 50) | UGX 45,000 | **4.2×** ✅✅ |
| Cargo pants | UGX 19,600–27,200 | UGX 60,000–90,000 | **2.4–3.3×** ✅ |
| Chinese/Cuban collar shirt | UGX 30,000–39,200 | UGX 45,000 | **1.15–1.5×** ⚠️ |
| Jeans (new) | UGX 42,300–45,300 | UGX 80,000–95,000 | ~2× |

**Read this carefully:**

1. **Tees, polos and cargo pants are where the money is** — 2.4–4× markups.
2. **The fashion shirts (Chinese/Cuban collar) have thin margins** at Nairobi stall prices. Only worth it if you negotiate to KSh 800–1,000 for volume, or source via Eastleigh at MOQ 50–100.
3. **The MOQ 50 rules matter.** The KSh 200–350 tee/polo price only unlocks at 50 pieces. That's KSh 10,000–17,500 (UGX 300,000–530,000) — a big chunk of your 1.5M for one line.
4. **⚠️ On the branded items** — the U.S. Polo Assn. and Timberland pieces in your screenshots. Genuine branded goods are not sold at UGX 45,000 wholesale. Items at these prices are style copies. Selling them *as* genuine brands carries legal risk under Uganda's counterfeiting laws. Sell them as unbranded fashion, or avoid the logo-heavy lines.
