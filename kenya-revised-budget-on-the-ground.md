## 2. The one calculation that matters: Uganda duty

Worst case on new clothing (non-EAC origin — most Eastleigh/Industrial Area stock is Chinese):

```
Import duty 35%  +  VAT 18% on (value + duty)
= 35% + (18% × 1.35) = 35% + 24.3%
= 59.3% of what you paid for the goods
```

**So every KSh 10,000 of stock becomes KSh 15,930 landed in Uganda** — if URA assesses it as commercial.

Reminder of the rules: the $2,000 traveller allowance covers **personal use only**. 45+ new garments is very unlikely to pass as personal. Above 50kg, or clearly commercial quantity, goes to the cargo terminal regardless of declared value.

**Legitimate ways to reduce it:** buy Kenyan-made (EAC origin) goods and ask for a **Certificate of Origin** — EAC-origin clothing enters Uganda at **0% duty** (VAT still applies). Few Eastleigh items qualify, but Kenyan garment factories exist and it's worth asking.
