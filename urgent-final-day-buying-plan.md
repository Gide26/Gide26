## Inspect before you pay — 5 minutes, saves the whole trip

You have no time for a wash-test, so do this at the counter:

1. **Open the bag/pack and pull 5–10 pieces at random** — not the ones on top.
2. **Collar:** squeeze it. Does it spring back or stay crushed? Crushed = it'll look bad after one wash, and you'll be stuck with 50.
3. **Stitching:** check the side seams and hem. Loose threads or gaps = reject.
4. **Colour:** compare pieces against each other. Uneven dye = reject.
5. **Weight:** a 240gsm polo should feel substantial, not papery. Thin ones won't hold shape.
6. **Sizes:** confirm the size spread. Ask for a mix — S/M/L/XL/XXL. All one size won't sell.

**Say this:** *"Naweza fungua mfuko niangalie kwanza?"* — Can I open the bag and check first?
**Then:** *"Bei ya wholesale kwa pieces 50 ni ngapi? Na kwa 100?"* — often it drops again at 100.
