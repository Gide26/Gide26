PLOT AT 100% - NEVER SCALE.

THE SETTING
The same arrangement and the same colours as the sign post panel: BCM on a
white band, MEDICAL CENTRE in white on navy, EDDWALIRO knocked out of a
full-width red block, OPEN 24 HRS between two arrows, then the navy foot
strip with 0788 323 815 - 0754 132 715 at the left and BUSSABALA at the
right. There is NO MOTTO on this piece.

MATERIAL AND FINISH - CONFIRM WITH THE PRINTER
PVC banner 510 g, matte finish, welded hem all round, eyelets at the pitch
above. These are industry-typical values, not a quotation, and the printer
should be allowed to substitute their own standard.
