# BCM Medical Centre — entrance door film set

Two things: photographic service panels for the glazed entrance doors of BCM Medical Centre, and a name-only sign post panel. Three doorways, eight panes each, printed on perforated one-way vision film on
doors 1 and 2 and on opaque white vinyl on door 3 — the street reads a poster, the clinic
still sees out of doors 1 and 2.

Built to the style of the door-sticker boards in the premises photographs: white logo plate
at the top-left, photograph full bleed, solid navy caption band at the foot with the service
name set large.

**Provisional.** The B monogram is a recreation, not the clinic's official logo. The doors
are drawn to the measured glazing supplied by the client; the plate and caption positions
should still be checked against the real leaves before the film is cut.

---

## The measured set

Every door is eight panes on four leaves: four panes at **900 × 500 mm** and four at
**900 × 400 mm**, all 900 mm high. The narrow 400 mm panes are the ones **in the middle of the
doorway, against the meeting stile**; the 500 mm panes sit at the two outer edges. So leaf A runs
wide then narrow and leaf B runs narrow then wide. The geometry is identical on all four doors;
only the subject matter and the film change.

| 2 | [`door-2/`](door-2/) | The welcome door — WELCOME (open 24 hours), immunisation, general outpatient, obs & gyn, minor surgery, pharmacy (medicine handed over the counter), HIV care | One-way vision | 7 photographs + directory |

Twenty-eight different photographs across the four doors — **no photograph is used twice**, and the
services are spread rather than repeated: pharmacy appears on doors 1, 2 and 4 (a strip of tablets in
hand, medicine handed across the counter, and a dispensary counter), HIV care on doors 1 and 2,
laboratory on doors 1 and 4, CBC on doors 1 and 4, ultrasound on doors 1, 3 and 4, minor surgery on
doors 2 and 3; every other service is pictured once. Door 4 exists because of a client instruction
and is drawn on the same measured door as the first three: it repeats no photograph from any other
door, and where it repeats a *service* the second pane is cropped and captioned differently. Every frame with people in it has been retouched so the
faces and hands read as Black African, matching the community BCM serves. Each door folder
carries its own artwork, schedule, specification, elevations, web preview, README and package zip.

## The sign post panel

A separate deliverable: **46 in × 22 in** as the client measured it (revised on 2026-11-06
from 16 ft 7 in × 49 in, which followed 4 ft × 2 ft and 3.10 × 2.11 ft), landscape, which is
**1168.4 × 558.8 mm**. No service list, no photograph, no motto. Double-sided; both faces are
in the one print PDF.

**Built to the sign the clinic already has.** The client sent a night photograph of the lit box
on the road and asked for the new face to be made like it, so the arrangement is the one people
there already read: `BCM` on a white band, `MEDICAL CENTRE` in white on navy,
`EDDWALIRO` knocked out of a full-width red block, an arrow at each end of the `Open  24 Hrs`
row, and a navy foot strip with `TEL 0788 323 815 · 0754 132 715` at the left and `BUSSABALA`
at the right.

Red `#ED1B24` still carries the flag — the block behind the name and the OPEN 24 HRS row — with
the clinic's navy and white around it; the two navy bands are thin and sit with white between
them, so the box lights evenly rather than through one dense field. The earlier cyan field is kept as
a spare.

them is a quotation.

## Photography — read this before printing

The base photographs are licensed Pexels stock (free commercial use, no attribution,
modification permitted — terms checked 29 October 2026). The frames that carry people were
retouched with an AI image editor: skin tone only, so the people read as Black African.
Scenes, poses, clothing, equipment and lighting are the original photographs. Every retouch
is recorded in the table in each door's README, and in the licence log in its schedule.

Two consequences, stated in every door document:

- The people are **models, not BCM staff or patients.** No pane names them or presents
  them as staff.
- The working files are **proof-resolution only** (500–1264 px on the long edge). They are
  right for approval and for the mockups, not for a printed 900 mm pane; each door README
  states the ppi the crops actually resolve to. For production, the retouch is repeated on
  the full-resolution original from the Pexels link recorded against each asset, or the
  clinic's own team is photographed.

## Identity

- Navy `#16233F`, cyan `#2BA9E0`, mist `#E8EEF4`
- Motto on the plate: *We listen. We treat. We care.* — set under MEDICAL CENTRE at a
  size that never competes with the name, and held to the name's width
- **EDDWALIRO** — the Luganda word for hospital — heads the sign post panel, above the
  badge: bold, cyan, same cap height as the English name, so the panel reads to the road in
  Luganda first while MEDICAL CENTRE keeps the widest line, in white. It is not on the door
  panes: at 400 mm there is no room for a second language beside the name
- B monogram: cyan arc around a bold B, drawn in vector (recreation, swap for the real logo)
- Plate: monogram + `BCM` over `MEDICAL CENTRE`. The name carries the weight — MEDICAL CENTRE
  is set at 2.6 × the cap height of the initials, in the dark navy, spanning the full plate, so
  it is the thing a passer-by reads and points at. The initials sit above it in cyan as a
  kicker. Slogan appears on panes 470 mm wide and over, where it still reads through the
  perforation; the pane number sits in the plate's foot in grey
- Caption band: service name at 13.5 % of the pane width, subtitle and help line beneath

## Banners

### The 46 × 22 in banner — the sign's own setting

`banner-1168x559/`, built in `source/bcm_banner_46.py`: one banner at the sign post's
measurement, **1168.4 × 558.8 mm (46 × 22 in)**, in the sign's colours and the same band
arrangement — `BCM` on white, `MEDICAL CENTRE` in white on navy, `EDDWALIRO` knocked out of the
red block, `Open 24 Hrs` between two arrows, and the navy foot strip with
`TEL 0788 323 815 · 0754 132 715` and `BUSSABALA`. No motto.

It is PVC on a fence, not a light box, so the artwork keeps **45 mm clear of the trim** for a
25 mm welded hem and eyelets at 300 mm centres, where the sign runs to the edge. Print files come
as an exact-size page (= 46 × 22 in) and a bleed version; there is a two-page proof, a
dimensioned sheet, a README and a zip.

### The three roadside banners

Three larger roadside banners on the fence, built in `source/bcm_banners.py`. All three were
re-measured on 2026-11-07 to **16 ft × 49 in (4876.8 × 1244.6 mm)**:

| ID | Size | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B-1 | 16 ft × 49 in (4876.8 × 1244.6 mm) | photograph left, the name and service list right |
| B-2 | 16 ft × 49 in (4876.8 × 1244.6 mm) | type left, two photographs stacked right |
| B-3 | 16 ft × 49 in (4876.8 × 1244.6 mm) | the name centred in its column, one photograph at the right hand end |

**Same design, the sign post's colours.** These three keep the layout they were approved
with — EDDWALIRO at the head, the badge and `BCM`, MEDICAL CENTRE as the widest line, the
motto, the service list, the numbers and OPEN 24 HOURS, photograph at the left on B-1, two
photographs stacked at the right on B-2, one at the right with the type centred on B-3.
Nothing moved, nothing was re-sized.

On 2026-11-07 the client asked for the sign post's colour to be copied onto them, so the
navy field and its cyan went and the sign's own colours came in — the same red `#ED1B24` and
navy `#16233F`, band for band, at the same ratio the sign uses (its band is 1.19 × the cap it
carries): white ground, EDDWALIRO in white on the red block, MEDICAL CENTRE in white on the
navy band, and that red again for the motto, the service rules and OPEN 24 HOURS. The badge,
the numbers and the service names sit in the navy. Because the red and the navy are the sign's
own values, fence and light box read as one set.

Three changes followed at the client's request later the same day, on top of the approved
layouts: the B badge came off entirely, so `BCM` stands alone; `BCM` is set large — 152 mm cap
on B-1 and B-2, 104 mm on B-3 — and centred; and every display line on all three banners is
now centred on the column's axis, the service list included, set three to a row with a red
rule before each name. The list stays dark blue throughout.

Each banner still carries a service list from the clinic's own board. Four Pexels frames, all
colour-retouched, all recorded on the licence page of `bcm/banners/BCM-Banners.pdf`. No
photograph and no service repeats across the three.

## Rebuilding

```sh
cd source
python3 bcm_doors.py          # all four doors — artwork, mockups, schedule, spec, web, zips
python3 bcm_doors.py 1        # one door
python3 bcm_sign.py           # the sign post panel — artwork, proof, elevations, web
python3 bcm_banners.py        # the three roadside banners
python3 bcm_print.py          # one print PDF per piece, into bcm/print/
python3 bcm_master.py         # the complete set: master proof, manifest, README, zip
python3 bcm_premises.py       # the set drawn in place on the building
```

Preview the whole set locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory bcm` and open
`http://localhost:8000/`. The hub at `bcm/index.html` links every door and the sign.

`bcm_brand.py` holds the identity and the white logo plate; `bcm_doors.py` the per-door
spec, crops and copy; `cp_photos.py` the licensed asset register; `cp_render.py`,
`cp_docs.py`, `cp_spec.py`, `cp_site.py` and `cp_mockup.py` are shared and switch identity
through `use_brand()`.

## Before release

1. Confirm the fascia wording — the board reads *Community Health Centre*, the artwork now uses
   *BCM Medical Centre* as instructed, with MEDICAL CENTRE as the dominant line.
2. Confirm the slogan — the board reads *Good Health is Your Responsibility*, the artwork
   reads *We listen. We treat. We care.*, the motto the client supplied.
3. Send the official logo file to replace the recreation. If the real logo already contains the
   name, the plate can be simplified to badge + name at a single size.
4. Confirm the help-line numbers and whether the email address should appear.
5. Re-run the retouch on the full-resolution originals, or photograph the real team.
6. Replace the flagged printer values (hole pitch, open area, bleed) with the printer's own
   standard, and proof on the actual film, not on paper.
