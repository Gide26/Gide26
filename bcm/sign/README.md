# BCM Medical Centre — sign post panel, for a lighting board

Name only. No service list, no photograph, no monogram.

**The post is going into an internally lit box, so the face is built light.** A bright cyan
field, EDDWALIRO and BCM in white, MEDICAL CENTRE in navy as the widest line, the motto under a
short rule. Navy is kept for the name alone: low ink coverage is what lets the tubes light the
panel evenly, and dense ink over a large area is what makes a backlit sign look patchy.

**EDDWALIRO** — Luganda for hospital — heads the panel: white on cyan, so the road reads it
first, and MEDICAL CENTRE keeps the widest line. Where the monogram used to sit, **BCM** now
stands in the display face, at the same weight as the rest of the block. The recreated monogram
is no longer on this sign.

**Trim: 1168.4 × 558.8 mm — the client's 46 in long × 22 in high, landscape.**
Revised on 2026-11-06 from 16 ft 7 in × 49 in, which itself replaced 4 ft × 2 ft
(1219.2 × 609.6 mm) and, before that, 3.10 × 2.11 ft.
Double-sided: both faces are in the one print PDF and are identical.

## Two fields — cyan ships, white is the spare

- **Cyan `#2BA9E0` field, white EDDWALIRO and BCM, navy `#16233F` name.** The recommended face.
  It glows hard behind a diffuser and keeps the identity colour on the sign.
- **White field, cyan EDDWALIRO, navy BCM and name.** The brightest option, and the one that
  still reads properly if the box is ever switched off. Ask for it if the box is dim.

Both are the same lockup; only the colours change.

## Measurements

- Trim 1168.4 × 558.8 mm (46 in × 22 in at 25.4 mm to the inch; 3 ft 10 in × 1 ft 10 in)
- Bleed 10 mm all round → 1188.4 × 578.8 mm, plus a 22 mm slug at the foot → page 1188.4 × 600.8 mm
- Safe area 25 mm in from trim
- Both faces identical; print the two-page file once and turn the sheet
- Plot at 1 : 1. The file is drawn in millimetres; do not scale it.

## Production — confirm with the signwriter, none of this is a quotation

- **Backlit-grade face.** Translucent diffusing acrylic or a backlit film for the box's own
  face, or backlit banner media if the box takes a soft face. Not vinyl on opaque composite —
  that blocks the light.
- Print the cyan field as one solid, even coat. No gradient, no vignette: banding in a big flat
  field shows far more when it is lit from behind.
- Double-sided: the artwork is identical on both faces, so the same file prints twice.
- **The face fits the box's aperture, not its outer frame.** The visible opening should be the
  trim size above, or the rebate will crop the artwork.
- Panel centre 1500 mm above finished ground level is a proposal — check it against the box and
  the kerb line.
- If it also stands on posts rather than in the box: two 76 mm galvanised posts at 520 mm
  centres, 600 mm into concrete, is the industry-typical proposal.

## Before plot

1. Check 46 × 22 in against the box's aperture.
2. Ask for a printed sample of the cyan field **lit from behind** before the full run — backlit
   cyan reads cooler and brighter than the same ink on paper.
3. Confirm the spelling EDDWALIRO before plot.
4. The help-line numbers (0771 240 104 / 0754 132 715) are off the clinic's premises boards;
   they can be added at the foot on request.
