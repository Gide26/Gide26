THIS FACE IS FOR AN INTERNALLY LIT BOX
The field is plain white and the words carry the sign: EDDWALIRO and MEDICAL
CENTRE set edge to edge in red, BCM between them. White is the field that
lights most evenly, so the box glows quietly and the red lettering is what
shouts - that is the instruction behind this version. Print on backlit-grade
media: translucent diffusing acrylic or backlit film, or backlit banner media
if the box takes a soft face. Vinyl on opaque composite blocks the light.

RED IS THE ONE COLOUR TO WATCH IN A LIGHT BOX
A red this bright will bleed slightly into the white when it is lit from
behind - the letter edges soften and the red can warm towards orange. Two
things fix it: keep the tube close behind the face and even, and ask the
printer to hold the red as a flat spot colour rather than four-colour build
if the process allows. Check a lit sample before the full run.

THE SETTING
EDDWALIRO across the full measure in red, BCM in red under it, MEDICAL CENTRE
across the full measure in red, a short red rule, the motto in deep red.
No monogram. Every line is set as wide as the panel allows, so the type is as
large as 46 x 22 in will carry.

ONE THING TO CHECK BEFORE THE RUN
The face must fit the box's aperture, not its outer frame: the visible opening
should be the trim size above, or the rebate will crop the lettering.

STRUCTURE - CONFIRM WITH THE SIGNWRITER
