FACE A IS THE APPROVED SETTING (OPTION A)
EDDWALIRO heads the panel, the badge sits under it, BCM above MEDICAL CENTRE,
the motto at the foot. This is the arrangement approved on the earlier sample,
kept at this length. It sits centred with wide navy margins either side -
that is what the proportions do at 16 ft 7 in.

OPTION C is the alternative: the same elements turned on their side, badge at
the left, EDDWALIRO / MEDICAL CENTRE / motto run beside it. The name is about
four times the size and fills the length. Use it only if the client asks -
it is not the approved setting.

STRUCTURE - CONFIRM WITH THE SIGNWRITER
The face is about 6.29 m2. 3 mm ACM over this span is marginal: ask for 4 mm
ACM or 3 mm ACM on a rail frame, four posts, or a cleat frame. Panel centre
1500 mm above finished ground is a proposal only. Nothing here is a quotation.
The monogram is a recreated mark, not the clinic's official logo.
