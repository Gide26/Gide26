THIS FACE IS FOR AN INTERNALLY LIT BOX
The field is bright on purpose: a lit sign is read as light, not as ink, and a
dark face in a light box comes up patchy and grey. Navy is used only for
MEDICAL CENTRE, the widest line, so the ink coverage stays low and the tubes
light the panel evenly. Print on backlit-grade media - a translucent diffusing
acrylic or backlit film, or backlit banner media if the box takes a soft face.
Vinyl on opaque composite will block the light.

CYAN IS THE RECOMMENDED FIELD
EDDWALIRO in white, BCM in white under it, MEDICAL CENTRE in navy, the motto
in navy under a short rule. The monogram is gone - BCM stands in its place.
The white-field file is the same layout on white: it is the brightest option in
the box, and it is the one that still reads with the light switched off.

ONE THING TO CHECK BEFORE THE RUN
The face must fit the box's aperture, not its outer frame: the visible opening
should be the trim size above, or the rebate will crop the artwork. Ask for a
printed sample of the cyan field lit from behind before the full run - backlit
cyan reads cooler and brighter than the same ink on paper.
