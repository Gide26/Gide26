THIS FACE IS FOR AN INTERNALLY LIT BOX
The face is the arrangement of the sign already standing on the road, brought
up to date: BCM on a white band, MEDICAL CENTRE in navy on a light blue band,
EDDWALIRO knocked out of a full-width red block, OPEN 24 HRS between two arrows,
and a navy foot strip with the numbers and BUSSABALA.
The navy sits in thin bands with white either side, so the box lights evenly.
Print on backlit-grade media: translucent diffusing acrylic or backlit film, or
backlit banner media if the box takes a soft face. Vinyl on opaque composite
blocks the light.

RED IS THE ONE COLOUR TO WATCH IN A LIGHT BOX
A red this bright will bleed slightly into the white when it is lit from
behind - the letter edges soften and the red can warm towards orange. Two
things fix it: keep the tube close behind the face and even, and ask the
printer to hold the red as a flat spot colour rather than four-colour build
if the process allows. Check a lit sample before the full run.

Top band, white: BCM, centred. Then the light blue band MEDICAL CENTRE in navy.

EDDWALIRO knocked out of it in white at the largest size the panel will take.

Then the navy foot strip: the numbers at the left, BUSSABALA at the right.

WHICH FILE TO USE
For the box: send the EXACT file if the face is being made to the panel size,
or the bleed file if the printer wants bleed and crop marks. They are the same
artwork - only the page edge differs.

ONE THING TO CHECK BEFORE THE RUN
The face must fit the box's aperture, not its outer frame: the visible opening
should be the trim size above, or the rebate will crop the lettering.
