Production notes

Film

  • Perforated one-way vision vinyl, white-backed, 140–160 µm plus liner.
  • 1.6 mm holes on a 2.3 mm staggered pitch — about 44 % open by geometry.
  • Matte anti-glare face; UV or latex print, four colour plus white backing.
  • Every figure here is industry-typical and flagged confirm with printer.

Artwork

  • Trim 810 × 1120 mm (five panes) and 810 × 1180 mm (three panes).
  • 10 mm bleed, 25 mm live margin, 20 mm slug, crop marks outside bleed.
  • Service names at 54–66 mm cap height — sized to survive a 44 % perforation.
  • Nothing live below 6 mm; no caption ever sits on a photograph.
  • Photographs audited in the placed crop: 77–172 ppi, all above the 75 ppi floor — on perforated film the 2.3 mm hole pitch is the real limit.

Identity

  • Colours sampled from the client's fascia sign: navy #16233F, cyan #2BA9E0.
  • Slogan and both telephone numbers transcribed from the client's own board.
  • Manrope display / Inter text, both open licence.
  • No invented services, hours, accreditations or claims.

Photography & licence

Real, professionally shot stock photography as the base — no AI-generated scenes or people. 6 of the originals then carry a colour retouch, made with an AI image editor so the people reflect the community the clinic serves; scenes, poses and clothing are untouched. The people are models, never staff or patients, and no panel captions them as such.
