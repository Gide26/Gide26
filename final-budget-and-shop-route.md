*"Nataka bei ya wholesale — nataka pieces thelathini. Bei gani?"*
Then **walk away at least once per shop.** Twenty shops sell the same stock. Walking is your strongest move.

**Inspect every bag before paying:** squeeze the collar — does it spring back? Check side seams and hems. Compare 3–4 pieces for even colour. **Ask for mixed sizes** (S/M/L/XL/XXL) — all one size won't sell.
