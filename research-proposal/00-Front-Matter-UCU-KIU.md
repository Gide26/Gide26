# FRONT MATTER TEMPLATE (UCU / KIU / Ugandan University Format)

> Assemble in this order. Confirm with your department: (a) APA 6th vs 7th, (b) whether a student pilot needs UNCST clearance or a faculty exemption letter, (c) abstract length (usually 250–300 words), (d) binding template (title-page wording, apron colours).

## Order of pages
1. **Title page:** ASSESSING THE IMPACTS OF NEW VISION OVER THE PAST 40 YEARS OF ITS EXISTENCE (1986–2026) / BY / [FULL NAME] / [REG. NO.] / A Research Proposal/Dissertation Submitted to [Department, School, University] in Partial Fulfilment of the Requirements for the Award of [Degree] of [University] / [Month, Year: e.g., October 2026]
2. **Declaration** (candidate: original work, never submitted elsewhere; signed, dated)
3. **Approval** (supervisor(s) + Head of Department; signed, dated)
4. **Dedication** (optional, brief)
5. **Acknowledgement** (supervisor, librarians, 3 KIIs anonymised, family, funder if any)
6. **Table of Contents** (auto-generated in Word; 3 heading levels)
7. **List of Tables / List of Figures** (numbered per chapter: Table 4.3, Figure 4.1…)
8. **List of Abbreviations/Acronyms** (starter: ACME, ABC, BUK, FES, FGD, KII, LG Luganda, NVL, NVPPCL, REC, RSF, UCC, UNCST, UPE, USE)
9. **Abstract** (template below — write LAST, after Chapter 5)
10. **Chapters 1–5** + References + Appendices (instruments, clearance letters, coverage log, sample coded sheets)

## Abstract template (~250 words — fill after fieldwork)
> Background (2 sentences: 40 years, no longitudinal assessment). Purpose + 4 objectives (3 sentences). Methodology (pilot mixed-methods: 100 editions / n=40 / 3 KIIs / desk review; sites; analysis; 3 sentences). Key findings (one sentence per objective — desk-led, preliminary). Conclusions + recommendations (2 sentences) + full-study roadmap (1 sentence).

## Formatting checklist (typical UCU/KIU)
- [ ] Font Times New Roman 12, 1.5 spacing (check dept: some require double), justified body
- [ ] Headings numbered (1.1, 1.1.1); each chapter starts on a new page
- [ ] Tables/figures numbered, titled ABOVE tables / BELOW figures, sourced underneath
- [ ] In-text citations + reference list match 1:1 (no orphans either direction)
- [ ] Page numbers: roman (i, ii…) for front matter, arabic from Chapter 1
- [ ] Turnitin/similarity check per university policy before submission
