## INSTRUMENT 3: KII Guide (3 interviews × 30–45 min)

**Consent (read + sign):** voluntary; recording [ ]Y [ ]N; attribution [ ]anonymous [ ]role-only [ ]named. **KII-__: Role __ | Date __ | Duration __ | Mode [ ]in-person [ ]remote**

1. When did you first encounter New Vision? Walk me through the period you know best.
2. In that period, what was New Vision's political role? Give one example each of strong and compromised coverage.
3. Elections: from what you observed, how did New Vision cover incumbents vs. opposition? (Probe: 2016/2021 if known.)
4. Best development contribution (health/education/unity)? What evidence convinced you?
5. How did money shape coverage — adverts, government supplements, expos? Any boycott/pressure episode you can share?
6. Who was included/excluded by language and distribution choices?
7. When did digital "hit" New Vision? What worked, what failed, why?
8. Compared to Monitor/UBC, where does New Vision stand today — and why?
9. What must change for New Vision to thrive to 50 (2036)?
10. What documents/people must this study not miss? May I send you my 1-page summary to check?

*Role probes:* Lecturer → theory fit (Schramm? Herman & Chomsky? Entman?) + student uses. Veteran journalist → newsroom routines then vs now; story-kill example (anonymised). NGO officer → monitoring data pointers; reform demands.
