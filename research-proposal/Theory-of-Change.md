# THEORY OF CHANGE (ToC): New Vision's 40-Year Impacts

> The explicit causal story this whole research tests. Insert the diagram + pathway summary into **Chapter One (§1.8, beside the conceptual framework)** and use the "pilot test" column to show examiners exactly which table tests which link.

## Vision statement

**IF** a state-majority but commercially operating national media house (New Vision) produces sustained political, development, and multilingual content over 40 years, **THEN** it shapes Uganda's public agenda and democratic discourse, builds media-industry and advertising capacity, improves health/education knowledge and national cohesion, and — contingent on digital adaptation — sustains these roles into the online era, **BECAUSE** audiences use its content for decisions and identity (Uses & Gratifications), elites and citizens take issue cues from its agenda (Agenda-Setting), its frames define problems and solutions (Framing), its development content teaches and mobilises (Development Media Theory), **BUT** all of this is bounded by ownership and advertiser pressures (Political Economy) and by who is offline, non-literate, or non-covered (digital divide).

## Four impact pathways (one per objective) + assumptions

**P1 — Political pathway (Obj.1; Agenda-Setting + Framing + Political Economy).** Sustained coverage → issues become publicly salient → citizens/elites act → discourse quality shifts. *Assumptions:* (A1) audiences exposed; (A2) coverage minimally credible; (A3) opposition/critical voices not fully excluded. *Risk:* state ownership collapses A3 (bias → agenda capture). **Pilot test:** Tables 4.3–4.5 (issue/actor/stance shares) + ACME 2016/2021 secondary + survey trust/fairness items.

**P2 — Economic pathway (Obj.2; Picard/Doyle + Political Economy).** Scale + credibility → advertiser demand → revenue → jobs, printing value chain, talent pipeline → industry capacity. *Assumptions:* (A4) ad market grows with economy; (A5) commercial logic doesn't corrupt editorial firewall. *Risk:* digital unbundles classifieds/display (Picard, 2014). **Pilot test:** §4.4 desk (circulation, NVL, advertiser footprint 2010→2026) + KII commercial testimony.

**P3 — Social/development pathway (Obj.3; Development Media Theory + Uses & Gratifications).** Health/education/unity content → knowledge → behaviour/practice → welfare gains. *Assumptions:* (A6) content actionable and comprehensible; (A7) audiences motivated and able to act; (A8) multilingual reach covers diversity. *Risk:* top-down messaging without participation (Servaes/Wasserman critique). **Pilot test:** thematic coding (tone/actionability) + survey recall items D4/D5 + health/education KII.

**P4 — Digital-sustainability pathway (Obj.4; Pavlik/Beckett + Wasserman).** Platform investment → online reach/engagement → digital revenue → survival of P1–P3 impacts. *Assumptions:* (A9) connectivity/affordability rise; (A10) audiences pay or advertisers follow online; (A11) newsroom converges without quality collapse. *Risk:* 28% penetration + rural youth majority → reach without revenue (AMI, 2016). **Pilot test:** §4.6 evolution table (2010→2016→2026) + audit snapshot + willingness-to-pay items.

## Falsification rules (what would DISPROVE each pathway — state these to earn examiner trust)

- P1 fails if: opposition voices ≈0 across decades AND audiences report zero agenda/learning effect AND trust collapses → then New Vision is pure propaganda, not agenda-setter.
- P2 fails if: no employment/ad-market/training footprint is documentable → then it's economically extractive, not developmental.
- P3 fails if: health/education content is rare, non-actionable, or unrecalled → then development claims are rhetoric.
- P4 fails if: digital reach is negligible, unmonetised, and newsroom unconverted → then impacts die with print.
