## 6. Before you spend a shilling

1. **Go to Extreme Warehouse and buy ONE polo and ONE tee** (~KSh 550). Wash them. Check the collar holds shape and colour doesn't run. That KSh 550 protects your entire budget.
2. **Confirm the MOQ and price face-to-face.** Ask for the price at 50 and at 100 pieces — often it drops again.
3. **Check the bureau spread** on a small amount before changing your UGX.
4. **Keep KSh 4,000 for the bus** in a separate pocket. Do not touch it.
5. **Get a receipt** for the stock. URA may want to see value — and their own valuation may differ from what you paid either way.
