SIGNAL METHODOLOGY

How the score works

Northstar uses a simple, transparent rules-based score to summarize the sample metrics you provide. It is a screening aid, not a forecast.

Trend · 35%Price above the 20- and 50-day moving averages.
Momentum · 30%RSI range and MACD direction, with a penalty for overbought readings.
Participation · 20%Volume compared with its recent average (when supplied).
Price action · 15%Recent percentage change, capped to limit outlier influence.
Bring your own dataImport a CSV with columns ticker, name, asset, price, change, volume, rsi, macd, trend20, and trend50. Optional: volumeRatio. Use percent points for change (for example 2.4 for +2.4%), volume in millions, and true/false or 1/0 for MACD and trend fields. Import is processed in this browser.

Bullish / Watch / Bearish are classifications of these indicators only. Always verify data, consider risk, and do your own research.
