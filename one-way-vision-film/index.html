03

The system

One palette, two typefaces, one grid. Everything else is photography.

Palette

  • Navy#0A2642Ground field — 60% of every panel
  • Navy Deep#06182ALockup ground, shadows
  • Teal#0C8C8CAccent rules and labels
  • Medical Green#2E7A5FSecondary accent, used sparingly
  • Mist#DCE6ECBody text on navy
  • White#FFFFFFHeadlines

Typography

Manrope

Department names, 600–800. Geometric with open apertures — holds at 70–78 mm cap height and still reads through a 38% perforation.

Inter

Labels, indices and technical text, 400–600 with 0.15–0.25 em tracking. Never below 4 mm cap height anywhere on a panel.

Grid

  • 1250–2100 mm photographic band, full bleed to the head of the panel
  • 660–640 mm accent rule and department label
  • 372 mm headline baseline — identical on every panel
  • 140 mm kick zone: no live content below the lockup
  • 25 mm safe zone, 10 mm bleed (printer to confirm)
04

Designed for perforation

Drag the slider to see the film's 1.6 mm holes at true scale over the headline. This is what the patient actually sees from a metre away.

1.6 mm / 2.3 mm38% open area
  • Headlines never sit over photography — solid navy gives maximum edge contrast.
  • No stroke thinner than 0.3 mm; hairline rules carry no information.
  • Photography covers 40–45% of a door; sidelights carry none at all, preserving daylight.
  • The darkest zone is the most transparent: navy is the "window" of the panel.
  • From inside, the panel reads as a dark field — do not rely on it for privacy after dark.
